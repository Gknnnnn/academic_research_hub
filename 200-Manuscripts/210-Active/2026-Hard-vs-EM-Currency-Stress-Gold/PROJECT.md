# Hard vs. EM Currency Stress & Gold
## Objective
Compare gold's safe-haven role during hard-currency vs. emerging-market stress episodes.
## Data
Daily gold + USD index + EM basket (INR, BRL, ZAR, TRY), 1990-2024.
## Methodology
1. Regime-switching VAR (Markov)
2. Time-varying Sharpe ratio (crisis vs. normal)
3. Quantile spillover analysis
## Output
Q1 international finance / commodity journal.
## Status
Stage 2: Data sourcing; preliminary correlation matrices.
