# Multipolar & Basel Rules — Financial Regulation
## Objective
Model impact of Basel III/IV on financial stability in multipolar monetary order.
## Data
30+ banks + 20 countries, 2009-2024; regulatory capital ratios, NPL, liquidity coverage ratios.
## Methodology
1. Difference-in-Differences (Basel adoption timing)
2. Regulatory event study (announcements)
3. Multi-level mixed-effects model (bank + country + time)
## Output
Q1 financial regulation / development finance journal.
## Status
Stage 1-2: Conceptual scoping; data access agreements pending.
