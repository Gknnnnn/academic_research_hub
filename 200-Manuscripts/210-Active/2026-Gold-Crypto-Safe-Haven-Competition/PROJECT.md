# Gold vs. Crypto — Safe-Haven Competition
## Objective
Analyze whether Bitcoin/Ethereum displace gold as inflation hedge and safe-haven asset.
## Data
Daily gold, BTC, ETH prices + macros (inflation, VIX, USD index), 2015-2024.
## Methodology
1. Time-varying correlation (rolling window + DCC-GARCH)
2. Quantile comovement (QQ regression)
3. Safe-haven event study (tail risk episodes)
## Output
Q1/Q2 cryptocurrency finance or commodity journal.
