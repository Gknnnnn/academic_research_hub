## 🎯 PRIORITY COLLABORATION TOPICS

### TOPIC 1 — Technology, Labor Share & Economic Complexity [⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ TOP PRIORITY]
**Seed paper:** Ergül & Göksel (EconMod 2020) — "The effects of technological development on the labor share"
**MGO extension:** Add Economic Complexity Index (ECI), extend to Eurasian/OECD panel, apply CS-ARDL + CCEMG
**Hypothesis:** Technology → labor share effect is heterogeneous by economic complexity level (interaction term)
**Data:** OECD STAN / EU KLEMS / WB WDI / OEC ECI (Atlas)
**Method:** CS-ARDL + PMG + CCEMG; CSD test first; AMG for robustness
**Target:** Economic Modelling (SSCI Q2, IF≈7) — Göksel already published here ×3
**N:** ~25-35 OECD countries; T≈1995-2022
**Status:** IDEA — needs data pull and design

### TOPIC 2 — Financial Development → Current Account Dynamics in Türkiye [⭐⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed paper:** Göksel (EconMod 2012) — "Financial constraints and international trade patterns"
**MGO angle:** Extend from trade to full CA/ödemeler dengesi; use ARDL bounds + NARDL asymmetry
**Hypothesis:** Financial depth reduces CA deficit persistence; asymmetric response to tightening vs. easing
**Data:** CBRT, WB WDI, FRED (Türkiye quarterly, 1990-2024)
**Method:** ARDL bounds + NARDL (Shin et al. 2014); Bai-Perron breaks; Granger causality
**Target:** Journal of International Money and Finance (SSCI Q1) OR Economic Modelling
**Status:** IDEA — links to MGO ödemeler dengesi tezi (Bölüm 4)

### TOPIC 3 — Government Size, Inequality & Carbon Emissions [⭐⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed papers:** Elgin, Goksel et al. (EconMod 2013) gov.size + MGO's GovSize EKC (published, Uluslararası Akademik Birikim)
**MGO angle:** Add carbon/environmental quality; panel of emerging markets
**Hypothesis:** Government size × inequality interaction shapes EKC turning point
**Data:** WB WDI, EDGAR CO2, UNU-WIDER inequality
**Method:** Panel FE + Driscoll-Kraay; nonlinear threshold; System-GMM
**Target:** Environmental Science & Policy (SSCI Q2) OR Economic Modelling
**Status:** IDEA — MGO has methodology; Göksel brings theory + gov.size literature

### TOPIC 4 — Trade Policy Uncertainty & Export Diversification in Emerging Economies [⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed paper:** Çınar & Göksel (SBF Dergisi 2010) — "İhracatta bölgesel çeşitlendirme ve istikrar"
**MGO angle:** Add GPR (Caldara-Iacoviello) and trade policy uncertainty (Ahir et al. WUI); panel extension
**Hypothesis:** Geopolitical risk → export concentration (Herfindahl); asymmetric by trade regime
**Data:** BACI HS92 (CEPII), GPR index, WUI (IMF), WB
**Method:** Panel FE + IV (Bartik); Dumitrescu-Hurlin causality; PVAR
**Target:** Journal of International Trade & Economic Development OR International Economics
**Status:** IDEA — MGO has BACI data from gravity papers

### TOPIC 5 — Synthetic Control: Energy Policy Reform & Industrial Outcomes [⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed paper:** Öztürkler & Göksel (2015) Syrian refugees synthetic control
**MGO angle:** Apply SC to Turkish energy reform events (2009 renewables law, 2018 energy crisis)
**Hypothesis:** Renewables policy shock → measurable impact on industrial output / CA
**Method:** Synthetic Control (Abadie 2010) + inference via permutation
**Target:** Energy Policy (SSCI Q1) OR Energy Economics
**Status:** IDEA — requires defining treatment event precisely
