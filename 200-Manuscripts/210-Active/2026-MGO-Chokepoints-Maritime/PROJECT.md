# Chokepoints & Maritime Trade — Geopolitical Risk
## Objective
Quantify trade disruption & insurance-cost premiums during maritime chokepoint tensions.
## Data
Monthly container volumes (Strait of Hormuz, Bab el-Mandeb, Malacca), shipping rates, 2010-2024.
## Methodology
1. Event-study on geopolitical incidents (Houthi, Suez blockade 2021)
2. VAR spillover to energy/commodity prices
3. Gravity model with chokepoint-intensity weights
## Output
Q1 international trade / energy economics journal.
## Status
Stage 2-3: Data sourcing; incident timeline curation.
