# Central Bank Independence & Financial Stability
## Objective
Measure Central Bank independence (Alesina-Summers + legal index) → inflation, exchange stability.
## Data
25-30 developing countries, 1990-2023; CBI indices, inflation, FX volatility, debt, political risk.
## Methodology
1. Fixed-effects ARDL (CBI lags on inflation/FX stability)
2. IV/2SLS (instrument: colonial legal origin → CBI)
3. Threshold analysis (debt turning points)
## Output
Q1 monetary economics / development journal.
## Status
Stage 6: revision-ready manuscript + submission preparation.
