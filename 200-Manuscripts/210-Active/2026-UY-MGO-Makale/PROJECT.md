# Uruguay-MGO Paper — Sustainability Nexus
## Objective
Country-case study: Uruguay's agricultural sustainability & migration-carbon links.
## Data
Uruguay national accounts (2000-2023) + agricultural survey + migration stocks (remittances).
## Methodology
1. ARDL cointegration (agriculture → carbon; migration → growth)
2. Granger causality (structural VAR)
3. Policy scenario modeling (2050 targets)
## Output
Q1 regional/development journal or Uruguay-focused regional journal.
## Status
Stage 2-3: Data assembly; baseline VAR specification.
