# Regime Volatility & Gold Forecasting
## Objective
Model gold returns under macroeconomic regime switches (Markov-switching).
## Data
Daily gold, macroeconomic uncertainty indices (EPU, VIX, CESI), 2000-2024.
## Methodology
1. Markov-switching GARCH (3-state: stable, uncertain, crisis)
2. Regime-conditional forecasts (conditional volatility)
3. State-dependent Sharpe ratio (risk-adjusted returns)
## Output
Q1 commodity/finance journal.
## Status
Stage 2: Model specification + regime identification.
