# Target Switching in Currency Wars — Predictors
## Objective
Forecast central bank FX intervention target switches (from EUR to CNY, etc.).
## Data
Central bank reserves, FX flows, bilateral rates (EUR/USD, USD/CNY, etc.), 2000-2024.
## Methodology
1. Multinomial logit (target choice: EUR vs. CNY vs. other)
2. Leading indicators (trade share, capital flows, geopolitical risk)
3. Event-study on actual target switches (historical episodes)
## Output
Q1 international finance / political economy journal.
## Status
Stage 2: Target switch timeline construction; reserve data harmonization.
