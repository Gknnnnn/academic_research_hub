# Sustainability Nexus — Uruguay Regional Case
## Objective
Integrate sustainability dimensions: agriculture-energy-carbon-migration-growth (Uruguay + LA comparator).
## Data
Multi-country LA (Uruguay, Brazil, Argentina, Chile), 2000-2023; matched indicators.
## Methodology
1. CS-ARDL with cross-section dependence (Pesaran CD, CIPS pre-test)
2. Heterogeneous slope coefficients (AMG)
3. Mediation via agricultural productivity or FDI green sectors
## Output
Q1 sustainability/development journal.
## Status
Stage 2: Comparative data assembly; Pesaran CD pre-tests.
