# AgTFP, Energy Efficiency & Carbon — MENA/SSA Frontier
## Objective
Assess whether agricultural TFP growth reduces energy intensity and carbon across climate-vulnerable regions.
## Data
22 countries (5 frontier + 7 MENA + 10 SSA), 2000-2020; DEA-efficiency scores.
## Methodology
1. Two-stage DEA (Malmquist index)
2. Panel econometrics: CS-ARDL, Pesaran CD/CIPS
3. Mediation analysis (TFP → energy intensity → carbon)
## Output
Q1 agricultural economics / environmental journal; full DEA diagnostics.
