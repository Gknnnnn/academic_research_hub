# Gravity Model & Food Security — Trade Network
## Objective
Model bilateral food trade networks & identify critical supply-chain nodes threatening food security.
## Data
BACI HS92 food products (1995-2024) + FAO food balance sheets.
## Methodology
1. Structural gravity (PPML) with product differentiation
2. Network centrality metrics (eigenvector, betweenness)
3. Shock propagation model (supply disruption spillover)
## Output
Q1 international trade / food security journal; policy-brief potential.
## Status
Stage 2-3: BACI data sourcing; FAO food balance harmonization pending.
