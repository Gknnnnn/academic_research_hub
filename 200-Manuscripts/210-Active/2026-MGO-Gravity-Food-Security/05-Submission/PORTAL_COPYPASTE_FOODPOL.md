## Highlights (copy exact — all ≤85 chars ✅)
1. Agri-food distance elasticity is −0.75 and sector-symmetric (p = 0.755).
2. Distance friction shows no trend over 2005–2020 (p = 0.266).
3. FTA raises perishable trade 108% vs. 46% for durables (Δ = +0.366, p < 0.001).
4. FTA asymmetry is robust to endogeneity (BYZ F = 1.45, p = 0.234).
5. Institutional depth — not infrastructure — drives fresh-food trade security.
