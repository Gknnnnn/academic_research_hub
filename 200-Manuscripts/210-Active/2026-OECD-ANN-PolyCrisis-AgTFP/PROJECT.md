# Polycrisis & Agricultural TFP — OECD
## Objective
Quantify agricultural productivity loss during overlapping crises (pandemic, energy, inflation).
## Data
OECD + non-OECD countries, 2018-2024; AgTFP (USDA), energy prices, supply shocks.
## Methodology
1. VAR with exogenous crisis shocks (structural breaks)
2. Impulse response functions (orthogonal + recursive)
3. Tail-risk concentration (Expected Shortfall)
## Output
Q1 agricultural / resource economics journal; OECD policy brief potential.
## Status
Stage 2: Data harmonization (OECD AgTFP + Eurostat).
