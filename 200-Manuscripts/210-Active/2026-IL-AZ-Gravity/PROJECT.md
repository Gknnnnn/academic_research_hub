# Israel-Azerbaijan Gravity Model
## Objective
Estimate bilateral trade flows & normalization impact using gravity framework.
## Data
Bilateral trade (HS92 COMTRADE), 1995-2024; distance, FTA dummies, geopolitical events.
## Methodology
1. Poisson pseudo-maximum likelihood (PPML)
2. Structural gravity with multiple equilibrium effects
3. DiD on normalization shock (2020/2022)
## Output
Q1 international trade journal; policy implications for Levant.
## Status
Stage 3: Data assembly + baseline PPML estimation.
