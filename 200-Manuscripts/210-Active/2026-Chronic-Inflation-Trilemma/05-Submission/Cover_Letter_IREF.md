I submit for your consideration the manuscript:

**"Trilemma Configuration and Chronic Inflation Traps in Developing Economies: Panel Evidence from 106 Countries, 1985–2020"**

**Contribution.** This paper tests whether a country's position along the Mundell-Fleming impossible trinity dimensions — monetary independence (MII), exchange rate stability (ERS), and capital account openness (KAOPEN) — is a robust predictor of *chronic inflation*, defined as CPI growth exceeding 10% for three or more consecutive years. Using a panel of 106 developing and emerging economies over 1985–2020, I find that exchange rate stability is the dominant anti-inflation trilemma instrument (APE = −19.8 pp, RE Probit), while monetary independence raises chronic inflation risk (APE = +18.3 pp) — an empirical signature of the Barro-Gordon time-inconsistency mechanism. These results are confirmed by System-GMM (ERS b = −2.095***, AR(2) p = 0.578) and IV/2SLS using the Shambaugh (2004/2019) base-country peg as instrument (FS-F = 174.43; IV-FE ERS = −0.910***). The IV estimates exceed OLS in absolute magnitude, confirming attenuation bias from reverse causality.

**Fit with IREF and gap addressed.** While the literature documents that individual trilemma dimensions each affect inflation outcomes (Mendonça & Tiberto 2017, IREF; Montes et al. 2024, IJFE), no published study tests all three Aizenman-Chinn-Ito indices simultaneously against a *binary chronic-inflation indicator* in a large developing-economy panel with IV identification. Continuous-inflation regressions obscure the threshold mechanism at which discretionary monetary policy generates self-reinforcing inflationary traps — precisely the Barro-Gordon reversal recovered here. This paper fills that gap: N = 106 economies over 1985–2020 is the largest sample in any single-outcome trilemma study, and the positive MII coefficient (+18.3 pp APE) — absent from prior published panel evidence — constitutes the primary novel contribution. The paper's multi-estimator design (RE Probit + FE Driscoll-Kraay + System-GMM + IV/2SLS + CIPS panel unit root) exceeds the methodological bar of the standard IREF macro panel paper.
