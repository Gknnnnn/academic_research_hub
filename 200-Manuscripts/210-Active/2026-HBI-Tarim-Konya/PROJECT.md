# HBI Agricultural Floor — Konya
## Objective
Assess human behavioral index (HBI) as predictor of agricultural yield & sustainability.
## Data
Konya province, agricultural output + climate + HBI indices, 2000-2023.
## Methodology
1. ARDL bounds cointegration
2. VECM Granger causality
3. Regional heterogeneity analysis (intra-Konya districts)
## Output
Q1 regional/agricultural economics journal.
## Status
Stage 3-4: Data assembly ongoing; overlaps with Konya-HBI-Agricultural-Floor.
