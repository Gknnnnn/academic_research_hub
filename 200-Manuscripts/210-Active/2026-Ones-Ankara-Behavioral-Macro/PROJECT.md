## 🎯 PRIORITY COLLABORATION TOPICS

### TOPIC 1 — Central Bank Credibility, Transparency & Exchange Rate Pass-Through [⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ TOP]
**Seed:** Öneş CBI + inflation targeting (Türkiye) × MGO'nun Currency Misalignment / NARDL portföyü
**Hypothesis:** Higher CB transparency → lower ERPT; asymmetric (depreciation vs. appreciation)
**Data:** CBRT survey (inflation expectations), BIS ERPT estimates, WB WDI, IMF IFS (2003-2024)
**Method:** NARDL (Shin et al. 2014) + MS-VAR for regime changes; Bai-Perron breaks at TCMB değişimleri
**Target:** Economic Modelling (SSCI Q2) OR International Review of Economics & Finance (SSCI Q2)
**Why now:** MGO's currency papers (IERFM/EconMod) already in pipeline — natural extension
**Status:** IDEA — strong theoretical bridge

### TOPIC 2 — Behavioral Monetary Policy Uncertainty & Household Dollarization [⭐⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed:** Öneş behavioral/experimental + MGO dedolarizasyon EPFAD paper (v01 2026-04-23)
**Hypothesis:** Monetary policy uncertainty → dollarization through behavioral salience channel (attention model)
**Data:** CBRT EDDS (döviz mevduat oranı), Bloomberg monetary surprise, Türkiye inflation expectations survey
**Method:** NARDL + structural break; behavioral channel proxied by news sentiment (NLP) or survey
**Target:** Journal of International Money and Finance OR Emerging Markets Review
**Status:** IDEA — Öneş behavioral expertise provides novel "why" for dollarization persistence

### TOPIC 3 — Monetary Policy Asymmetry & Current Account Adjustment [⭐⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed:** Öneş "Asymmetric Effects of Monetary Policy: Evidence from Turkey" × MGO ödemeler dengesi tezi
**Hypothesis:** Contractionary vs. expansionary monetary policy → asymmetric CA adjustment path
**Data:** CBRT policy rate, WB CA/GDP, TCMB EVDS (quarterly 1990-2024)
**Method:** NARDL + MS-ECM; test for asymmetry in speed of adjustment; Wald nonlinearity test
**Target:** Economic Modelling OR Applied Economics Letters (short paper, fast track)
**Status:** IDEA — directly complements MGO's Bölüm 4 of ödemeler dengesi tezi

### TOPIC 4 — Experimental Evidence on Inflation Expectations Formation [⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed:** Öneş experimental methodology (JEBO public goods) → apply to monetary policy communication
**Hypothesis:** CB communication framing affects inflation expectation anchoring differently across literacy groups
**Design:** Lab experiment (Ankara Üni. öğrencileri) — treatment = forward guidance framing
**Target:** Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization (Öneş already published here) OR Experimental Economics
**Status:** IDEA — longer-term, requires lab setup; fits "pioneer" profile

### TOPIC 5 — Public Goods Provision & Environmental Taxation Compliance [⭐⭐⭐]
**Seed:** Öneş collective action experiments + MGO environmental economics
**Hypothesis:** Earmarked environmental tax framing → higher compliance vs. general tax (lab experiment)
**Target:** Ecological Economics OR Journal of Environmental Economics and Management
**Status:** DISTANT IDEA — methodologically innovative but requires experimental infrastructure
