# Gold Policy Uncertainty — Repricing Effects
## Objective
Quantify gold repricing elasticity to geopolitical & policy uncertainty shocks.
## Data
Gold prices (daily), 2000-2024; EPU index, VDAX, GPR index.
## Methodology
1. ARDL bounds cointegration
2. Asymmetric NARDL (positive vs. negative shocks)
3. Frequency-domain Wavelet coherence (short vs. long-term spillover)
## Output
Q1 financial markets or commodity journal; policy-uncertainty nexus.
## Status
Stage 2-3: Literature + preliminary data inspection.
