# ZRY × MGO — South-South & Uruguay Framework
## Objective
Analyze South-South trade, migration, carbon dynamics linking Zimbabwe (ZRY), Mongolia, Uruguay.
## Data
Bilateral trade (COMTRADE), migration (UN), carbon (WDI), 1990-2023; TZA comparator.
## Methodology
1. Structural gravity (PPML) with South-South dummy
2. VAR spillover (trade volume → prices → consumption)
3. Panel mediation (trade liberalization → carbon via income effect)
## Output
Q1 trade/development journal.
## Status
Stage 1-2: Conceptual scoping; case selection finalization.
